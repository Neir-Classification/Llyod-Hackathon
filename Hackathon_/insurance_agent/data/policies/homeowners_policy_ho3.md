# SECTION I - PROPERTY COVERAGES

## COVERAGE A - DWELLING

We cover the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations, including structures attached to the dwelling, and materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises used to construct, alter or repair the dwelling or other structures on the residence premises.

### Covered Perils for Dwelling
We insure against direct physical loss to property described in Coverage A, except as excluded in Section I - Exclusions.

---

## COVERAGE B - OTHER STRUCTURES

We cover other structures on the residence premises set apart from the dwelling by clear space. This includes structures connected to the dwelling by only a fence, utility line, or similar connection.

**Coverage Limit**: 10% of Coverage A limit

### What We Cover
- Detached garages
- Storage sheds
- Fences and retaining walls
- Driveways and walkways
- Swimming pools (in-ground)
- Gazebos and pergolas

---

## COVERAGE C - PERSONAL PROPERTY

We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. At your request, we will cover personal property owned by others while the property is on the residence premises.

### Special Limits of Liability

The following limits apply to certain categories of personal property:

| Property Type | Limit |
|--------------|-------|
| Money, coins, bank notes | $200 |
| Securities, deeds, manuscripts | $1,500 |
| Watercraft including trailers | $1,500 |
| Trailers not used with watercraft | $1,500 |
| **Jewelry, watches, furs, precious stones** | **$1,500 total** |
| Firearms | $2,500 |
| Silverware, goldware | $2,500 |
| Business property on premises | $2,500 |
| Business property off premises | $500 |
| Electronic data processing equipment | $5,000 |

**IMPORTANT**: To insure jewelry, watches, or other valuable items for their full appraised value, you must add a **Scheduled Personal Property Endorsement** (also known as a "floater" or "rider"). This endorsement provides:
- Agreed value coverage (no depreciation)
- Broader coverage including mysterious disappearance
- No deductible typically applies
- Worldwide coverage

---

## COVERAGE D - LOSS OF USE

If a covered loss makes the residence premises uninhabitable, we cover:

1. **Additional Living Expenses**: The necessary increase in living expenses to maintain your normal standard of living.
2. **Fair Rental Value**: The fair rental value of the residence premises less expenses that do not continue.
3. **Civil Authority**: If civil authority prohibits use of the residence due to a covered loss to neighboring premises, we cover Additional Living Expenses and Fair Rental Value for up to 2 weeks.

# SECTION I - PERILS INSURED AGAINST

## Covered Perils

We insure against direct physical loss caused by the following:

### 1. Fire or Lightning
All direct loss caused by fire or lightning is covered.

### 2. Windstorm or Hail
We cover damage from windstorm or hail, but we do not cover:
- Rain, snow, sleet, sand or dust damage to the interior unless wind or hail first damages the roof or walls

### 3. Explosion
All explosion damage is covered.

### 4. Riot or Civil Commotion
Damage from riots or civil commotion is covered.

### 5. Aircraft
Damage caused by aircraft, including self-propelled missiles and spacecraft.

### 6. Vehicles
Damage caused by vehicles, but only when the vehicle is not owned or operated by a resident of the premises.

### 7. Smoke
Sudden and accidental damage from smoke is covered, excluding smoke from agricultural or industrial operations.

### 8. Vandalism or Malicious Mischief
We cover vandalism and malicious mischief, but:
- If the dwelling has been vacant for more than 60 consecutive days, this coverage is suspended

### 9. Theft
We cover theft, including attempted theft and loss of property from a known place when it is likely that the property has been stolen.

**Theft Exclusions:**
- Theft by an insured
- Theft from a dwelling under construction
- Theft from any part of the residence rented to others (unless the insured is residing there)

### 10. Falling Objects
Damage from falling objects is covered if the falling object first damages the exterior of the building.

### 11. Weight of Ice, Snow, or Sleet
We cover damage caused by the weight of ice, snow, or sleet to property contained in a building.

### 12. Accidental Discharge of Water or Steam
We cover accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a:
- Plumbing system
- Heating system
- Air conditioning system
- Automatic fire protective sprinkler system
- Household appliance

**We do not cover:**
- Damage to the system or appliance from which water escaped
- Damage caused by continuous or repeated seepage over 14 or more days

### 13. Sudden and Accidental Tearing Apart, Cracking, Burning, or Bulging
We cover sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of:
- Steam or hot water heating system
- Air conditioning or automatic fire protective sprinkler system
- Water heater

### 14. Freezing
We cover freezing of plumbing, heating, air conditioning systems, and household appliances, but only if you:
- Maintain heat in the building, OR
- Shut off the water supply and drain all systems

### 15. Sudden and Accidental Damage from Artificially Generated Electrical Current
We cover damage from power surges, but not to tubes, transistors, electronic components, or circuitry that is part of appliances or other equipment.

### 16. Volcanic Eruption
We cover direct loss caused by volcanic eruption, excluding earthquakes, land shock waves, or tremors.

# SECTION I - EXCLUSIONS

## What We Do Not Cover

We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by any of the following:

### A. Ordinance or Law
We do not cover loss caused by the enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating construction, repair, or demolition of a building.

### B. Earth Movement
We do not cover damage caused by:
- Earthquake
- Landslide
- Mine subsidence
- Mudflow
- Earth sinking, rising, or shifting

*Exception: Direct loss by fire, explosion, or theft resulting from earth movement IS covered.*

### C. Water Damage

**We do not cover damage caused by:**

1. **Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, tsunami, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these, whether or not driven by wind**

2. **Water or sewage from outside the residence premises plumbing system that enters through sewers or drains, or water which enters into and overflows from within a sump, sump pump, or related equipment**

3. **Water below the surface of the ground, including water which exerts pressure on, or seeps, leaks, or flows through a building, sidewalk, driveway, patio, foundation, swimming pool, or other structure**

**IMPORTANT NOTE ON WATER BACKUP COVERAGE:**
Standard homeowners policies DO NOT cover water damage from:
- Sump pump failure or overflow
- Sewer or drain backup
- Water seeping through foundation

**To obtain this coverage, you must add the WATER BACKUP ENDORSEMENT (HO-04-95 or equivalent).**

### D. Power Failure
We do not cover loss caused by the failure of power or utility service if the failure occurs off the residence premises.

### E. Neglect
We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss.

### F. War
We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution.

### G. Nuclear Hazard
We do not cover nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.

### H. Intentional Loss
We do not cover loss arising from any intentional or criminal act by an insured.

### I. Governmental Action
We do not cover seizure or destruction of property by government authority, except for actions taken to prevent the spread of fire.

# SPECIAL EXCLUSIONS - IMPORTANT READ CAREFULLY

## Mold, Fungus, and Wet or Dry Rot

### What Is NOT Covered
We do not cover loss caused by:
- Mold, fungus, or wet or dry rot
- Any condition that promotes mold growth

### Exception - Hidden Water Damage Mold Coverage
We WILL cover mold damage up to $5,000 if ALL of the following conditions are met:

1. The mold results from **sudden and accidental** water damage that would be covered under this policy
2. The water damage was **hidden from view** (behind walls, under floors, etc.)
3. The damage is reported to us **within 14 days** of discovery
4. You took reasonable steps to mitigate damage upon discovery

**Example of Covered Mold:**
A hidden pipe inside a wall slowly leaks, causing mold behind the drywall. The homeowner discovers it when removing wallpaper and reports it the same day. This would be covered.

**Example of NOT Covered Mold:**
Mold growing in a bathroom due to poor ventilation and high humidity. This is a maintenance issue and is excluded.

## Business Use Exclusion

### Commercial Activity Exclusion
We do not cover loss or liability arising from **business activities** conducted on the residence premises, including:

- Home-based businesses (without proper endorsement)
- Day care operations
- **Short-term rental activities (such as Airbnb, VRBO, or similar services)**

### Short-Term Rental Specific Exclusion
**IMPORTANT:** If you rent your home or any portion of your home to others for compensation, even occasionally:

1. **Standard theft coverage is VOID** for theft by or caused by renters/guests
2. **Liability coverage is VOID** for injuries to renters/guests
3. **Property damage caused by renters** is NOT covered

**To cover short-term rental activities, you must:**
- Notify your insurer of rental activity
- Purchase a **Landlord/Rental Dwelling Policy**, OR
- Add a **Short-Term Rental Endorsement** (if available)

## Tree, Shrub, and Plant Coverage

### What We Cover
We cover trees, shrubs, plants, and lawns on the residence premises for loss caused by:
- Fire or lightning
- Explosion
- Riot or civil commotion
- Aircraft
- Vehicles not owned or operated by a resident
- Vandalism or malicious mischief
- Theft

### Coverage Limits
- Maximum of 5% of Coverage A for all trees, shrubs, and plants
- Maximum of $500 for any single tree, shrub, or plant

### Tree Damage Liability
**Who is responsible when a tree falls?**

1. **Your tree falls on your property**: Covered under your policy if caused by a covered peril (wind, ice, etc.)

2. **Your tree falls on neighbor's property**: The neighbor files a claim on THEIR policy. Your liability may apply if the tree was dead/diseased and you knew about it.

3. **Neighbor's tree falls on your property**: YOU file a claim on YOUR policy. The neighbor is only liable if the tree was clearly dead/diseased and they were negligent.

4. **City/Municipal tree falls on your property**: File a claim on YOUR policy. The city is typically only liable if they were notified of a dangerous condition and failed to act.

# WATER BACKUP ENDORSEMENT (HO-04-95)

## This Endorsement Modifies the Policy

When this endorsement is attached to your policy, the following coverage is added:

### Coverage Provided
We will pay for direct physical loss to covered property caused by:

1. **Water or sewage** that backs up through sewers or drains
2. **Water that overflows** from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment

### Coverage Limit
The maximum we will pay for all loss under this endorsement from any one occurrence is the limit shown in the Declarations, typically:
- $5,000 standard
- $10,000 or $25,000 available for additional premium

### What This Endorsement Does NOT Cover
- Flood damage (requires separate NFIP policy)
- Continuous or repeated seepage
- Damage occurring while the dwelling is vacant for more than 60 days
- Failure to maintain the sump pump

# SECTION II - LIABILITY COVERAGES

## COVERAGE E - PERSONAL LIABILITY

We will pay damages an insured becomes legally obligated to pay because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence covered by this policy.

### Coverage Limit
The limit shown in the Declarations is the most we will pay for all damages from one occurrence.

### Defense Coverage
We will defend any suit for damages covered by this policy, even if the suit is groundless, false, or fraudulent.

---

## COVERAGE F - MEDICAL PAYMENTS TO OTHERS

We will pay necessary medical expenses incurred within three years from the date of an accident causing bodily injury to others.

### Who Is Covered
- A person on the insured location with permission of any insured
- A person off the insured location if injury arises from conditions on the insured location

### Who Is NOT Covered
- Any insured
- Any person eligible to receive workers' compensation
- Any person residing on the insured location

# CONDITIONS

## Duties After Loss

In case of a loss to covered property, you must:

1. **Give prompt notice** to us or our agent
2. **Protect the property** from further damage
3. **Cooperate with us** in the investigation
4. **Prepare an inventory** of damaged personal property
5. **Provide records** and documents we request
6. **Submit to examination under oath** if required
7. **Submit a signed, sworn proof of loss** within 60 days

## Your Duties After Loss - Time Requirements

| Action | Time Requirement |
|--------|-----------------|
| Report the loss | As soon as practicable |
| Protect property from further damage | Immediately |
| Submit proof of loss | Within 60 days |
| File suit against us | Within 2 years of loss |

---

## Loss Settlement

### Dwelling and Other Structures
We will pay the cost to repair or replace with like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.

### Personal Property
We pay the actual cash value at time of loss, unless you have **Replacement Cost Coverage** endorsement.

### Pair or Set
In case of loss to a pair or set, we may:
- Repair or replace any part to restore the pair or set
- Pay the difference between the value before and after the loss

---

## Subrogation

After we pay a claim, we may require you to assign to us your rights of recovery against others.

## Other Insurance

If other insurance covers the same loss, we will pay our proportionate share.

## Policy Period

This policy applies only to loss occurring during the policy period shown in the Declarations.

# DEFINITIONS

**"Bodily injury"** means bodily harm, sickness, or disease, including required care, loss of services, and death.

**"Business"** includes trade, profession, or occupation.

**"Insured"** means you and, if residents of your household:
- Your relatives
- Any other person under age 21 in your care

**"Insured location"** means:
- The residence premises
- Premises used in connection with the residence premises
- Premises newly acquired by the named insured
- Individual or family cemetery plots or burial vaults
- Land owned by or rented to an insured on which a dwelling is being built

**"Occurrence"** means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general harmful conditions.

**"Property damage"** means physical injury to, destruction of, or loss of use of tangible property.

**"Residence employee"** means an employee of an insured whose duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises.

**"Residence premises"** means the dwelling and grounds at the location shown in the Declarations where you reside.
